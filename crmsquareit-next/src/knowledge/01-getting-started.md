# Getting started with Squareit CRM

## What Squareit CRM is
Squareit CRM is the internal system Squareit staff use to run the agency: leads and quotations (sales), customers, invoices and payments, projects and tasks, support tickets, HR (attendance, leave, payroll), office expenses, announcements and reports. Everyone logs in with their own email and password at the login page.

## Logging in
1. Open the CRM website and enter your work email (for example you@squareit.in) and password.
2. Click Log in. You land on your Dashboard.
If you see "Invalid email or password", check the spelling of the email and that Caps Lock is off. If you see "Your account is inactive", your account was deactivated: contact HR or a Super Admin.

## Forgot password
1. On the login page click "Forgot password?" under the password field.
2. Enter your email and submit. If email sending is set up you get a reset link; the link expires after a short time.
3. Open the link and set a new password (at least 6 characters).
You can request at most 3 resets in 15 minutes ("Too many reset requests. Wait 15 minutes"). If no email arrives, ask HR or a Super Admin: your request shows as "Reset requested" on the Staff page and they can set a new password for you.

## Changing your password or profile
Click your name at the top right to open [My profile](/admin/profile). There you can change your first name, last name and phone, and use "Change password" (enter the current password, then the new one twice). New passwords need at least 6 characters.

## Logging out
Click the log-out icon at the top right, next to your name.

## Finding your way around
- The left sidebar lists every section you have access to, grouped as CRM (Leads, Customers, Quotations), Sales (Invoices, Tax invoices, Estimates, Payments, Expenses, Office expenses), Work (Projects, Tasks, Team work, Support) and Admin (HR portal, Reports, Staff & roles, Settings). You only see the sections your role allows.
- Dashboard, Announcements and My HR are visible to everyone.
- On a phone, tap the menu button at the top left to open the sidebar.
- Red number badges on the sidebar show things waiting for you: unread announcements, open support tickets, pending leave requests (HR) and submitted office-expense months (Super Admin).

## Search
The search bar at the top of every page searches customers, leads, invoices and projects. Type a name, email, phone number or invoice number and press Enter. If nothing is found, try a different spelling or a phone number.

## Quick create (+ New)
The "+" button in the top bar opens a quick menu to create a Lead, Customer, Invoice, Estimate, Project, Task, Ticket or Expense from any page. You only succeed if your role allows creating that item.

## The dashboard
Your dashboard depends on who you are:
- Super Admin sees the Control panel (company KPIs, departments, announcements, activity). HR sees the HR dashboard version of it.
- BDE (business development) staff see the BDE dashboard with their lead flow, follow-ups due today and recent quotations.
- Everyone else sees the standard dashboard: outstanding invoices, payments this month, leads, the payments vs expenses chart, the leads pipeline, "My open tasks", overdue invoices and latest activity (if allowed).
Super Admin and HR can open the older dashboard with the "Classic dashboard" link.
The greeting at the top says Good morning / afternoon / evening based on your own device's time.

## My to-do list
The "My to-do list" box on the dashboard is your private checklist. Type a to-do and press Enter to add it, tick it when done, or delete it. Your TL, HR and Super Admin can see your to-dos on the Team work page.

## Dates and time zone
All dates in the CRM ("today", "this month", overdue, follow-ups due) follow India time (IST), whatever the server's clock says.
