# Tasks, projects and team work

## Create (schedule) a task
1. Go to [Tasks](/admin/tasks) and click "Add task" (or + → Task, or "Add task" inside a project, or open [/admin/tasks/new](/admin/tasks/new)).
2. Fill in Task name (required), Description, Priority (Low, Medium, High, Urgent), Start date and Due date (this is how you schedule it), Status, and optionally Project / Customer and Billable.
3. Choose Assignees (who will do it):
   - Executives can only create tasks for themselves; they are assigned automatically.
   - TLs can assign to themselves and the Executives in their team ("You can assign tasks to yourself and the Executives in your team").
   - Super Admin and HR can assign anyone.
4. Super Admin / HR can also add Follow-ups (watchers): people who can see the task and track it without being assigned.
5. Save. The task opens on its own page.
If you see "Task name is required", fill in the name. "You can't edit this task" means you don't manage it.

## Task statuses
Not started → In progress → Testing → Awaiting feedback → Complete. Assignees and the task's managers can change the status from the task page. Completing a task records when it was completed and updates the project's progress.

## Who can edit a task
The person who created it, their TL, HR and Super Admin can edit details, reassign or delete. People assigned to it can still change its status and comment.

## Overdue tasks
A task whose due date is before today (India time) and isn't Complete shows in red as overdue on the Tasks list, dashboards and Team work.

## Comments and time tracking
On a task page, write in "Add a comment…" to discuss it. Use the timer to log time on the task: start it when you begin and stop when you finish; only one timer runs at a time, so starting another task's timer stops the first. Logged time shows on the task and project.

## Find tasks
The Tasks list has "My tasks", a search box, and filters for project, priority, status (Open tasks) and person (Everyone).

## Projects
1. Go to [Projects](/admin/projects) → "Create project".
2. Enter Project name and Customer (both required), Status (Not started, In progress, On hold, Cancelled, Finished), Start date, Deadline, Billing type (Fixed price or Hourly rate) with Total price / hourly rate, Description and Members.
3. Inside a project you see its tasks (with a Kanban view), time logged, progress, expenses and invoices. "Invoice project" creates an invoice for it.
Progress updates automatically from completed tasks.

## Team work page
[Team work](/admin/team) is for Super Admin, HR (everyone) and TLs (their Executives). Pick a person on the left to see their open tasks, overdue count, work finished in the last 30 days and their to-do list. Filter by department. "Open in Tasks" jumps to the full list. If a TL sees "No Executives are in your team yet", HR needs to place people in their department.
