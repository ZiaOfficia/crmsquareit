# Customers

## Add a customer
1. Go to [Customers](/admin/customers) and click "Add customer" (or + → Customer).
2. Fill in Company name (required), phone, website, GST / VAT number, address, city, state, ZIP / PIN, country and currency (leave "System default" to use the company currency).
3. Optionally add the Primary contact (first name and email are required for a contact).
4. Save.
Customers are also created automatically when a lead is converted.

## Customer page tabs
A customer page shows company details, contacts, notes, and tabs for quotations (Won quotations), invoices, estimates, projects, support tickets and payments. From it you can Create invoice, Create estimate, Create project and New quotation.

## Contacts
Add contacts to a customer with first name, last name, email, phone and position; one is the Primary contact. "First name and email are required."

## Record a customer payment (advance, full, part)
1. Open the customer → Payments tab ("Record a payment").
2. Pick the payment type: Advance (paid up front, before work starts), Paid at once (the full amount in one payment), Part payment (an instalment in between) or Final balance (the remaining amount). Enter the amount (for Paid at once and Final balance it defaults to what is outstanding), date, payment mode and optionally the transaction / cheque number and a note.
3. Optionally link it "Against quotation".
4. Save ("Payment recorded").
The customer shows Total billed (from accepted quotations), Paid and Outstanding. When everything is paid it says "Fully paid. Nothing is outstanding." If Total billed is empty, mark the client's quotation as Accepted, or convert their lead.

## Deactivate or delete
Deleting a customer that has invoices or payments deactivates it instead ("Inactive") to keep financial history.
