# Announcements and support tickets

## Read announcements
Updates from management appear as a banner at the top of the CRM, in the bell / inbox in the top bar, and on the [Announcements](/admin/announcements) page. Open one to mark it read, or mark all as read. Urgent and pinned announcements stay at the top.

## Send an announcement (Super Admin and HR)
1. Go to [Announcements](/admin/announcements).
2. Write a Title (under 160 characters) and the message.
3. Under "Send to" choose everyone, specific departments, or specific people.
4. Optionally mark it urgent, pin it, or set an expiry date (it stays visible until the end of that day, India time).
5. Send ("Announcement sent").
Open a sent announcement to see Read receipts: who has read it and who hasn't. HR can edit or delete only the announcements they sent; a Super Admin can manage all.
Errors: "Pick at least one department", "Pick at least one person", "The expiry date is in the past", "The selected departments have no active members yet".

## Support tickets
[Support](/admin/tickets) tracks client support requests.
1. Click "Open ticket" (or + → Ticket).
2. Enter Subject and Message (required), pick the Customer and Contact (or type a name and email if not a contact), Department, Priority, Project and who to Assign to.
3. Reply from the ticket page with "Send reply" and change its status: Open, In progress, Answered, On hold, Closed.
The sidebar badge counts open tickets. Support departments are set in Settings.
