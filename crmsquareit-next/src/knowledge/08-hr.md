# HR: attendance, leave, holidays and payroll

## My HR (everyone)
[My HR](/admin/my) is your own HR page. It shows today's attendance, your leave balances and requests, upcoming holidays and your payslips.

## Check in and check out (mark your attendance)
1. Open [My HR](/admin/my) when you start work and click Check in (or Check in WFH if you work from home that day).
2. Click Check out when you leave.
Check-ins after the office start time (set by HR, for example 10:00) show as late. If it says "It's your weekly off" or the day is a holiday, no check-in is needed. If you forgot to check in, ask HR to mark your attendance.

## Apply for leave
1. Open [My HR](/admin/my) → "Apply for leave".
2. Pick the Leave type: Casual leave, Sick leave, Earned leave or Unpaid leave.
3. Pick the From and To dates (or tick Half day for a single date) and write a reason.
4. Submit. It goes to HR as Pending ("Leave request sent to HR").
Rules: weekly offs and holidays aren't counted; you can't overlap another pending/approved request; you can't take more paid leave than your balance ("Only N day(s) of casual leave left. Apply the rest as unpaid leave"). Balances reset every calendar year. Unpaid leave has no limit but is deducted in payroll. You can cancel your own pending request.

## Leave statuses
Pending, Approved, Rejected, Cancelled. Approved leave is written into attendance as "On leave".

## HR portal (HR and Super Admin)
The [HR portal](/admin/hr) shows Present today, Late today, On leave, Not marked, pending Leave requests, who is on leave this week, and coming-up birthdays, work anniversaries and holidays.

## Mark attendance (HR)
In [Attendance](/admin/hr/attendance), mark a person Present, Work from home, Half day, On leave or Absent for a date, or use the quick buttons on the today board. "Mark the rest present" marks everyone without an entry as present. The Monthly register shows the month grid (P, W, H, L, A).

## Approve or reject leave (HR)
Open [Leave](/admin/hr/leave). Pending requests show with Approve / Reject. HR can also "Add leave for someone" (for example a phoned-in sick day); leave added by HR is approved straight away. HR can cancel any leave, which removes it from attendance.

## Holidays (HR)
In [Holidays](/admin/hr/holidays) add a date and name (for example Diwali). Holidays aren't counted as working days for leave or payroll.

## Employees (HR)
[Employees](/admin/hr/employees) holds each person's HR record: employee code, designation, gender, date of birth, date of joining and monthly (gross) salary. Salaries here drive payroll; birthdays and joining dates feed "Coming up".

## Payroll (HR)
1. Open [Payroll](/admin/hr/payroll), pick the month and click "Generate payroll" (once generated, the button becomes "Recalculate from attendance").
2. For every active employee with a salary, pay is worked out from attendance: per-day pay = monthly salary ÷ working days (weekly offs and holidays excluded). Loss of pay (LOP): Absent = 1 day, Half day = 0.5 day, unpaid leave = 1 day.
3. Add a Bonus or Deduction per payslip if needed; Net payable = salary − LOP + bonus − deduction.
4. Mark payslips Paid (one by one or the whole month). A paid payslip can't be changed until it's marked unpaid again. Recalculating never touches paid payslips.
Staff see their payslips in My HR once salary is paid.

## HR settings (HR)
In [HR settings](/admin/hr/settings): Office start time (check-ins after it are late), weekly off days, and paid leave per year for casual, sick and earned leave ("Change allowances").
