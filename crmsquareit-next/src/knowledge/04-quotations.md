# Quotations

## What quotations are
Quotations are priced proposals the BDE team sends to leads and customers, downloadable as a branded PDF. They live in [Quotations](/admin/quotations). BDE staff have full quotation access. BDE Executives see only quotations they created; BDE TLs and Super Admins see all.

## Create a quotation
1. Go to [Quotations](/admin/quotations) and click "New quotation" (or open a lead or customer and click "New quotation" there, which links it automatically).
2. Fill in the sections:
   1. Quotation for: the Service / project (for example "Website Development") and Date / Valid until.
   2. Prepared for: pick the lead or customer under "For (lead or customer)". Picking one fills in the details; you can still edit them.
   3. Engagement overview: opening paragraph and short description.
   4. Services & pricing: add each service with a name and fee (each fee must be above 0). "Add a saved service" inserts one from Settings → Saved items / services. Add GST / tax if needed. The subtotal and total are worked out automatically.
   5. Scope of work (optional): section headings with "What's included", one point per line.
   6. Acceptance & next steps: the steps (title + what happens).
   7. Terms & closing: one term per line as "Label: text"; the label prints in bold.
3. Click "Create quotation".
Errors: "Add at least one service with a name", "Each service needs a fee above 0", "Who is it for? Add the client's name".

## Write a quotation with AI
In the quotation form, paste the client's requirements or your call notes into "Client requirements / call notes" and use the AI draft button. The AI writes the whole quotation in the house format; review and edit before saving. The polish option rewrites what's already in the form into the quotation format. If it says "The AI couldn't find a quotation in those notes", add what the client needs and the prices.

## Download the PDF
Open the quotation and click "Download PDF".

## Quotation statuses
Draft → Sent → Accepted or Declined. Expired is shown automatically when a Draft or Sent quotation passes its Valid until date.
- Mark it Sent when you send it to the client. For a lead, this moves the lead to "Quotation sent" and counts as contacting them.
- Mark it Accepted when the client agrees. An accepted quotation is locked ("This quotation was accepted, so it can't be changed. Duplicate it to make a new version").
- Accepted quotations count as the customer's "Total billed".

## Make a new version
Use "Copy as a new draft" (Duplicate) on a quotation. It gets a new number and today's date.

## Quotation dashboard numbers
The Quotations page shows This month, Waiting for reply (sent, not expired), Won this month and Win rate.

## Default text
The quotation number prefix (QT-), validity days, opening paragraph, steps, terms and closing line are set by a Super Admin in Settings → Quotations (BDE).
