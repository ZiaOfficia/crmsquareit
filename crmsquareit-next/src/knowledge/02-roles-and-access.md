# Roles, departments and access

## Who can do what (overview)
Access in the CRM comes from three things added together:
1. Super Admin flag: a Super Admin has full access to everything, always, and is never limited by department rules.
2. Role: each staff member can have a role (made in Staff & roles → Roles & permissions) that grants view / create / edit / delete on each module (Customers, Leads, Invoices, Estimates, Quotations, Payments, Expenses, Office expenses, Projects, Tasks, Support, Reports, Staff, HR portal, Settings).
3. Department: being placed in a department adds extra access on top of the role.
If a section is missing from your sidebar, or a page says you don't have access (Forbidden), your role and department don't include it. Ask a Super Admin (or HR) to give you access.

## Departments and team roles
The organisation has departments: HR, BDE, Website Development, SEO, GBP, Paid Advertising (with sub-departments SMM and SEM) and SMO. Inside a department each person is either a TL (Team Lead) or an Executive. Someone can belong to more than one department.

## What each department adds
- HR department (TL or Executive): Staff (full), HR portal (full), Tasks (full), Expenses (full), Office expenses (full), Invoices (view, create, edit), Payments (view, create), Customers (view, create), Projects (view). HR can also send announcements and place people in departments. HR cannot change Roles & permissions, Settings or the Super Admin flag.
- BDE department: Leads (view, create, edit), Customers (view, create, edit), Quotations (full), and the BDE dashboard.
- Every TL and Executive in any department: Tasks (their own tasks, see the Tasks guide).

## Who sees whose work
- Super Admin and HR see everyone's tasks and to-dos and get the Team work page.
- A TL sees their own work plus the Executives in the departments they lead, and gets Team work for them.
- An Executive sees only their own tasks (assigned to them or created by them), plus tasks they were added to as a follow-up.
- Staff not placed in any department see all tasks (older behaviour).
- BDE Executives see only their own leads (assigned to them) and quotations they created. BDE TLs see all leads and all quotations.

## Giving someone access (Super Admin)
1. Go to [Staff & roles](/admin/staff) → Roles & permissions and create or edit a role: tick view / create / edit / delete per module, then Save role.
2. Open the staff member, pick the Role, and save.
3. To add department access, place them in a department (Departments section on the Staff page) with team role TL or Executive.
Putting someone in HR gives them HR access. Only a Super Admin can make someone a Super Admin or edit a Super Admin.
