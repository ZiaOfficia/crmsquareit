# Office expenses

## What office expenses are
[Office expenses](/admin/office-expenses) are the day-to-day running costs of the office (rent, tea & snacks, printer ink…). HR records them and, at month end, submits the month to the Super Admin for approval.

## Add an office expense (HR)
1. Open Office expenses and use "Add office expense".
2. Enter Date (required, not in the future), Category (required), Amount (greater than zero), "What was it for?" (short description, under 160 characters), and optionally Paid to (vendor, shop or person), Paid by, Bill / receipt # and a Note.
3. Save ("Expense added").
Use the Previous month / Next month arrows to move between months. The page shows the month total, daily average, the biggest category, a comparison vs last month and a breakdown by category.

## Submit the month (HR)
At month end, in "Month-end report", add an optional note for the Super Admin and submit ("Submitted to the Super Admin"). The month is then locked until the Super Admin reviews it. You can't submit a month that hasn't started.

## Review a month (Super Admin)
Submitted months show with a badge on the sidebar. Open the month and either Approve it or Send it back with a note telling HR what needs fixing. A sent-back month unlocks for HR; it appears again once they resubmit.
