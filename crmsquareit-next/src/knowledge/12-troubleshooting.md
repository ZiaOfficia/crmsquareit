# Troubleshooting and common problems

## I can't see a menu item or page / "Forbidden"
Your role and department don't give you access to it. Ask a Super Admin to add the permission to your role, or ask HR / Super Admin to place you in the right department.

## I can't log in
- "Invalid email or password": check the email spelling and Caps Lock, or use Forgot password.
- "Your account is inactive": HR or a Super Admin must reactivate you.
- No reset email: ask HR or a Super Admin to set a new password for you from the Staff page.

## I can't edit a task
Only the task's creator, their TL, HR and Super Admin can edit details. If you're assigned, you can still change its status and comment.

## I can't assign a task to someone
Executives can only assign tasks to themselves. TLs can assign to their own Executives. If a person is missing from the list, HR must place them in your department.

## A lead doesn't show on my dashboard
BDE Executives only see leads assigned to them. Ask your TL to assign it to you ("Assigned to" on the lead).

## The quotation can't be edited
Accepted quotations are locked. Use "Copy as a new draft" to make a new version.

## Leave request refused
- "Those dates are all weekly offs or holidays": no working days were picked.
- "There is already a leave request for some of these dates": cancel or adjust the other request.
- "Only N day(s) … left": apply the rest as Unpaid leave.
- "A half day must be a single date".

## Payroll doesn't include someone
Payroll only includes active staff with a monthly salary set in HR → Employees. A paid payslip can't change until it's marked unpaid.

## AI quotation draft fails
"The free AI limit was reached": wait a minute and retry. "Couldn't reach the AI service": check the internet connection. Ask an admin if it keeps failing.

## Overdue / today looks wrong
All "today", overdue and "this month" logic uses India time. If something still looks wrong, check the due date on the item itself.

## Who to ask
- Access, roles, settings: Super Admin.
- Attendance, leave, payroll, departments, passwords: HR.
- Your tasks and team: your TL.
